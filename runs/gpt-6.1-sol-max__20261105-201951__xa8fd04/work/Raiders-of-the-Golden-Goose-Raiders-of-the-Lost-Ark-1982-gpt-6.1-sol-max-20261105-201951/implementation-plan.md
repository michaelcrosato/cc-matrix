# Raiders of the Golden Goose

Original pulp action-adventure vertical slice, inspired by the supplied Raiders of the Lost Ark (1982). No source research. Title selected from 50 silently generated candidates with node:crypto.randomInt.

1. A continuous, authored 3D expedition: jungle, grapple chasm, dart galleries, three sun-lens puzzle, rival hunters and guardian, idol balance challenge, boulder escape.
2. Three.js r180 WebGPURenderer, cel materials, ink geometry, procedural articulated characters and native AnimationMixer clips. Rapier 0.19.3 handles bodies, collisions, jumping, debris and the boulder.
3. Keyboard/mouse, standard gamepad with adaptive glyphs, touch assistance, 1–4 couch co-op. Shared-world AI Hub Protocol v1 with host authority and a dedicated-server path.
4. Procedural Web Audio soundtrack (music 10% by default), effects and gibberish. Clear objectives, checkpoint saves, journals, pause/settings, developer tuning, credits and 10-second attract replay.
5. A repeatable headless test command covers actual gameplay, failure/retry, input devices, audio, saves and the supplied AI Hub harness. Visual checks cover composition and readability. Build one HTML in the workspace root; save exit-interview.json beside it.
