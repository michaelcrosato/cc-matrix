# Runtime contracts

- Fixed arena 960×540; identical bounds across aspect ratios. Native E.Game res is explicit and expand is false. Full arena fits; unused portrait space holds DOM information and controls.
- World.step(1/120) is the only gameplay mutation loop. Drawing consumes no gameplay randomness. World PRNG state is explicit and snapshots exclude engine/DOM/audio objects.
- PlayerIntent: authenticated seat (from transport), monotonic safe sequence, finite unit movement/aim vectors, fire/boost/pulse booleans. Input expires after 60 simulation ticks. Basic thrust/laser never consumes boost energy.
- Lifecycle: title/join → briefing → play → retreat → upgrade → briefing → play → result. Hub countdown is inert and end is terminal. A solo wipe consumes the first two layers; the final wipe loses. Co-op redeploy only occurs while another pilot lives.
- Resolution order: base loss → full wipe → heat overflow → boss defeat → encounter completion. Each step resolves at most one outcome. Neither a transition nor upgrade simulates damage.
- Swept shot-circle collision chooses the nearest hit with stable array/ID order. Kill guards and removing consumed pickups prevent duplicate score/resource grants.
- Rank thresholds: 0 Cadet; 6000 Lieutenant; 14000 Captain; 28000 Wing Commander; 46000 Commodore; 70000 Star Marshal. Chain multiplier grows every five uninterrupted kills, caps at five, expires after seven seconds. Damage costs 80, redeploy 300, forced retreat 500 and resets chain. Held layers award 1800, forced extraction 350, successful beats 500, final boss 6000 plus 25 per remaining base percent.
- Nine ordinary-data encounters: min duration plus completion goal, bounded fallback. Add a wave by adding one data object, no collision/render/network edits. New threat kinds require their own definition/behavior/art. Validation rejects invalid entries, timing and kinds.
- Hub host advances toward AIHub.time via a single timer-driven engine advance loop; RAF only paints. Local overlays neutralize intent without pausing the world. Dedicated host uses E.Game headless and owns no pilot/rendering/audio unlock.
- Client input at 15 Hz; host small updates at 10 Hz; reliable recoverable complete keyframes every 1.3 seconds, chunked to 4000 characters, up to 12 parts, scheduled under a combined 18-message/sec cap. Routine packets are measured ≤1200 bytes. Epoch/tick/assembly guards reject stale/partial state. Quantisation affects replication only. Client movement presentation predicts and reconciles acknowledged intents; permanent gameplay is host-only.
- Hub round duration is respected. Short custom rounds compress encounter gaps without skipping mechanics; normal 12-minute rounds retain authored pacing. Time-up reports finite real run scores and a tied co-op finish once. After end no input, packets, world steps or sound.
- Settings and best score use engine safe storage. Attract, cheats and balance-modified runs cannot write normal best scores. Runtime/dev settings are bounded and finite.
