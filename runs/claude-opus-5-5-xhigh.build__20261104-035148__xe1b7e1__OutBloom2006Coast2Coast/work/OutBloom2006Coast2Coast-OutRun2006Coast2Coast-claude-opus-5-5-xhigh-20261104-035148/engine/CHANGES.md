# Engine changes for OutBloom 2006: Coast 2 Coast

Base: my-3D2dge v0.85.2 handoff package (commit 4390a98). `my-3d2dge.js` here is the pristine file with the changes
below applied by `engine/patch-engine.py` (reproducible: `python3 engine/patch-engine.py <pristine> my-3d2dge.js road-ob.js`).
`modules/road.js` is the new road module on its own (the same code as in `my-3d2dge.js`).

| # | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Road junctions and strands.** A segment may carry a split (`split: [s0, s1]`): the road becomes two surfaces centred at -s and +s half roads, with a gore between them (its own ground), lane marks kept off the overlap and a dividing line while they part. `draw()` takes `strands`: other roads whose segment 0 sits at `z0` of this road and `x0` half roads across; each bends on from this road's bend at its start (or from the camera when it started behind it) and fades out after `maxSegs`. Rows of every strand share a depth band, so grounds split between strands at their middles. `projectStrand()` projects onto a strand. Things may belong to a fork branch (`th.branch`). | The fork is the source's defining structure; the old module drew one road only. |
| E2 | **Road dressing.** Per-segment width factor (eased), lanes, grounds per side (`grass`, `sand`, `water` with glitter, `rows` for crop and flower rows, `cobble`, `snow`, `rock`), edge strips per side (`rail`, `wall`, `stonewall`, `cliff`, `hedge`, `facade` with windows and awnings), overhead spans (`arch`, `bunting`, `canopy`/`palms`, `gallery`/`snowshed`, `banner` for checkpoint and goal arches), `tint`, `lit`, an `onBand` hook for painting on the asphalt, sprites clipped behind crests, and a square-pixel camera (`focal`, `horizon`) instead of the separate x and y scales. | Coast roads need sea on one side, rails, cliffs, arcades and arches; the old projection stretched x by width/height. |
| E3 | **Analog input.** `input.padValues` holds every pad button's value; `input.analog(action)` returns the largest of an action's pad values (the triggers), 1 for a held key; `input.setAnalog(action, v)` fixes it for tests and bots. | Throttle and brake on R2/L2 are progressive. |
| E4 | **Hub combo guard.** While `AIHub.active`, Options (button 9) is ignored while Create (button 8) is held. | The AI Hub protocol reserves Create + Options for the hub's corner bar. |
| E5 | **Glyph setting.** The standard `glyphs` setting defaults to `playstation`, with Xbox as the other choice (`input.glyphs` defaults the same). | TASK.md: PlayStation symbols, with a Settings option for Xbox. |
| E6 | **Synth extension.** `E.INSTRUMENTS` (name -> function scheduling a note) used by a track's `inst`; chord tokens (`C4+E4+G4`), accents (`C4!`), slides (`C4~`); drum tokens `p r g G b z m y T` (clap, rim, conga low/high, cowbell, shaker, timbale, ride, high tom; case kept); per-track `send: { delay, reverb }` into a tempo-synced delay and a convolution reverb on the music; a reverb on effects and voices (`audio.reverb(amount, fade)`); a voice bus with `voiceVolume` (`audio.voiceBus`, `audio.sfxBus`); `audio.musicLevel(v)` for ducking under speech. | The series' music is lush jazz-funk that bare square waves can't carry, and the voices need their own mix. |
| E7 | **Continuous voices.** `audio.loop(spec)` returns `{ set(params, ramp), stop(fade) }` for oscillators and noise that run on (an engine note, tyres, wind, gravel, ambience), with a filter, a shaper, amplitude wobble and pan; silent while paused (`audio.loops(on)` too); a no-op headless. | The engine had one-shot sounds only, and a car needs a living engine note. |
| E8 | **Shell theme for 10 feet.** A theme with `tv: '1'` adds the `esh-tv` class: base font clamp(18-30 px), 64 px buttons, a 4 px focus ring, panels up to 900 px. | The standard pause, settings and controls screens must read at TV distance. |

Everything else (the sky, the pixel bloom and flare, the car rasterizer, every UI screen, the race, audio content) lives
in the game's own files under `src/`.
