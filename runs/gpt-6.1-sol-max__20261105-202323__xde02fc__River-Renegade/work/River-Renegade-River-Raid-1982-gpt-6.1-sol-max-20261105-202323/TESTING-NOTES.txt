Product defects found and fixed during development verification:
1. Browser default Enter handling could activate a newly focused menu twice.
2. Menu repeat could skip an item during a brief controller press.
3. The touch pulse button inherited the HUD's non-interactive hit testing.
4. Mixed terrain triangle winding caused alternating shading bands.
5. The boss scroll lock left most of the barge above the camera view.
6. Intact bridges could be bypassed, undermining the mission objective.
7. Retrying or warping into a later sector did not retain bridge progress.
8. Attract mode could leave its faster cruise speed in a new player run.
9. The radio subtitle could cover the player's jet.
10. A generated tanker could overlap a fuel dock, obstructing refueling.

One objective-breaking defect: bridge bypass. No remaining known failures.
A dedicated regression now proves an intact span stops route progress and
that shooting it permits progress. Victory also requires all three bridges.

Additional test infrastructure findings, excluded from the product count:
- The software WebGPU adapter requires combined Vulkan and ANGLE flags.
- Screenshot readback can be blank with valid WebGPU draw counters.
- Controller stubs must remain pressed/released across sampled animation
  frames; fixed short wall-clock waits are unreliable on a software GPU.
- A debug object originally reused 'screen' for both a getter and a method;
  the method is now called 'open'.

The full campaign uses developer autopilot and invulnerability to exercise
all authored content. Independent tests cover ordinary damage, death,
reserves, fuel exhaustion, refilling, weapons, pickups and physics contact.
Human difficulty assessment, physical gamepads, real mobile hardware and
human listening are unknown.
