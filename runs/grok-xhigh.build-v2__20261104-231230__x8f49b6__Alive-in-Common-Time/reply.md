**Alive in Common Time** is a self-contained browser surgical sim for 1–4 players, a playful riff on Trauma Center: Second Opinion.

- Open `Alive in Common Time-Trauma Center: Second Opinion-grok-4.7-xhigh-20261104-231230.html` in a browser via `file://` or `http://127.0.0.1`.
- Aim with the right stick or mouse, pick tools with the left stick or keys 1–8, and use R2 or left click.
- Start Story or Free Play from the title screen. PlayStation glyphs are the default; switch to Xbox in Settings.
