01

TAKE THE STICK

Fly with W A S D and aim with the mouse. Hold the right mouse button to steer. Arrow keys also aim.

PAD · Left stick flies. Right stick aims.
TOUCH · Left thumb flies. Right thumb aims.

02

MAKE YOUR SHOTS COUNT

Hold Space / left click to fire. Hold E for target assist. Shift burns boost; Q switches shields; R checks aft.

PAD · R2 photons · L2 assist · R1 boost
L1 shields · Circle aft view

Open the chart with M. Select a sector, then engage hyperwarp. Longer jumps use more energy. Defend stations before their distress clocks expire.

Cross confirm jump · Options pause
