Thumb Yoga is a self-contained browser game: a five-day island balance retreat where you lean to hold poses, breathe, and stack pebbles.

- Open `Thumb Yoga-Wii Fit-grok-4.7-xhigh-20261104-223656.html` in current Chrome or Edge. It needs WebGPU and works from `file://` with no network.
- Lean with the left stick, WASD or arrows, or a pointer-locked mouse. Q and E are the feet; Space breathes.
- Pause for settings, controls, and dev mode. Up to four players share one screen in Group Class.
