# Dodge ’Em (1980) research

Researched 2026-11-05 before implementation. This is an original homage, not a port.

## Primary sources

- Atari, official game instructions: https://atari.com/pages/dodgeem
- Original Atari CX2637 instructions transcribed by AtariAge: https://atariage.com/manual_html_page.php?SoftwareID=984
- Original manual scan: https://www.ataricompendium.com/archives/manuals/vcs/dodge_em.pdf

## Mechanics and progression confirmed by original instructions

The point-scoring car automatically travels counterclockwise around four nested lanes. A computer crash car travels clockwise and attempts a head-on collision. A collision costs one of three turns. Collecting a full field of dots gives an eight-point bonus and another field; a turn permits five fields. A second computer car arrives after two fields. The accelerator creates a second speed. A slow car may cross up to two lanes through an opening, and an accelerating car crosses one. The difficulty switch can double computer speed after the first and third bonuses; the other switch varies computer starting positions. The three game types are solo, alternating two-player scoring, and simultaneous scoring-car versus crash-car with roles exchanged after a crash. Red and green are the human player colors; blue denotes the computer. The original instruction text uses short, direct arcade instructions.

## Look and sound

The original manual depicts a fixed overhead square circuit with four nested roadways and openings at each cardinal midpoint. Collectible dots mark the lanes. Cars and field use simple saturated colors and a black playfield. It is a highly readable abstract maze-driving game, with score and remaining turns above the track. The manual explicitly confirms audible engine roars and crashes. Exact synthesis waveforms and timing are unknown.

Two secondary accounts corroborate a quiet motor buzz, dot ticks, collision crunch, and no continuous music: https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/atari2600/584686-dodge-em/reviews/175323 and https://retroarcadememories.wordpress.com/atari-2600/dodge-em/ . These descriptions were read, not independently heard. A no-commentary real-hardware gameplay recording was located at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=njjYZi-zcJ8 ; direct audio listening was unavailable in the headless environment. Music absence is therefore reported as secondary corroboration, not a new firsthand finding.

## Interpretation for Dodging Daisies

Retain auto-driving, counter-rotating pursuers, lane-opening timing, two speeds, a full-board collection goal, escalating rounds, and three chances. Recast the geometry as an ink-outlined miniature garden circuit with original toy vehicles and flower tokens. An original procedural groove defaults to 10% music volume. Procedural motor hums, pickup ticks, gear chirps, crash bursts, fanfares, and formant-filtered gibberish supply feedback. No original image or audio assets are copied.
