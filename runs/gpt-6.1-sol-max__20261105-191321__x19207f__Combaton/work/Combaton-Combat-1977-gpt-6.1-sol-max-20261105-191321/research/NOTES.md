Combat uses opposing tanks, biplanes and jets, rather than a roster of monsters.
Its 27 variations combine open fields, easy/complex mazes, clouds, guided and
straight missiles, rapid guns, bouncing shells, invisibility and formations.
Bank-only rules require a wall hit before scoring. Own shells do not score
against their owner. Firing, impacts and wall contact expose invisible tanks.
Clouds conceal aircraft but do not protect them. Aircraft wrap at screen edges.
Tanks and jets are top-down; biplanes are side-on. The original match timer is
136 seconds. Its presentation has flat contrasting vehicles and geometric
playfields; copy is brief, competitive and playfully boastful.

Combaton adapts those rules into five shorter rounds, adds independent aiming,
smoke/scanner/roll tricks, a solo opponent and a complete onboarding flow.
