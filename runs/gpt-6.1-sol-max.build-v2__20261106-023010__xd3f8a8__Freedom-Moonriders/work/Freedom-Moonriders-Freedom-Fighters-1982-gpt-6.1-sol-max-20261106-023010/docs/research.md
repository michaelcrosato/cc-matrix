# Research actually inspected

- Original official-rules transcription: https://odyssey2.info/library/manuals/us_freedomfighters/freedom-fighters.txt (downloaded and read). Confirmed contact rescues, purple crystals, two drives with hyperspace overriding conventional flight, Attack and cannon-free Evasion, mine-laying Pulsars, 20/5/1 base scores and increasing pressure.
- Visually inspected original screenshots https://odyssey2.info/i/screens/free-scr.gif and https://odyssey2.info/i/screens/free-scr2.gif. Black space, tiny blue side-on player, violet square captives, radial gold enemies and white mines; sparse, sharply separated silhouettes.
- The supplied recording https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxCPJ6YP19k is Classic Game Room's review of the Magnavox Odyssey 2 cartridge. Fetched its actual player metadata (title/description identify hardware), then inspected 66 sequential storyboard frames at five-second intervals. Frames show the cartridge/manual, matching Odyssey 2 black playfield, scrolling enemies, dense mine constellations and repeated ship reappearance. This is sampled visual inspection, not continuous video playback. Streaming audio was inaccessible; exact sounds, waveforms, cadence and instruments remain **unknown**. The game uses original synthesized audio.
- Planner's Electronic Games field-collapse observation is retained as a design anchor, not relabelled as my own recording evidence.

All assets, narrative, music and sounds in this build are original code. Reference images are research only and are not bundled as game art.
