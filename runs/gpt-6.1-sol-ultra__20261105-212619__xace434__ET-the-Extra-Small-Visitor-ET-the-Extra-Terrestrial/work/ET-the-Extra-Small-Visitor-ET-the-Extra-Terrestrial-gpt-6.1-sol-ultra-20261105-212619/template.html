About this little world

Right stick · Page Up / Page Down · swipe to read

An original, unofficial game inspired by the communicator hunt, candy and pursuit of Atari's 1982 E.T., and the tenderness, backyard ingenuity and moonlit wonder of Steven Spielberg's film. All game models, animation, music, effects and gibberish voices are made in code.
