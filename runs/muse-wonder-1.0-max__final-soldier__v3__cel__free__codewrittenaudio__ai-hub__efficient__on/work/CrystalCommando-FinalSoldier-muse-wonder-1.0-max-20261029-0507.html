★ Mission Briefing — Operation Crystal Dawn ★

Year 2391. The Bloom Armada has stolen the Star Crystal, the heart that keeps every colony sky blue. Without it, the dawn simply… forgets to arrive.

You are Captain Kira Vale, last pilot of the Crystal Commando. Your ship, the Paper Comet, is small, brave, and held together with hope and excellent rivets.

Fly north. Break the Armada. Bring the morning back. And Kira — do try to keep the crystal in one piece this time. Mostly one piece. Several large pieces are also acceptable.

Your kit: 4 crystal weapons (Vulcan, Laser, Wave, Missile) · Force pods · Dash · Bombs · Upgrade drafts between waves.

Squad Link — play with a friend in another tab

Both pilots open this file, pick the same room code, one taps Host, the other Join. Your ships meet as friendly ghosts: fly together, share the sky, chase your own scores. (In an AI Hub session, the hub runs the party instead — this menu steps aside.)

How to Fly

Move — Left stick / D-Pad / WASD / Arrows
Fire (hold) — Cross (✕) / Z / J / Space
Bomb — Circle (○) / X / K
Dash (brief shield) — R1 / Shift / L
Focus (slow, tight fire) — L1 / C
Swap weapon — Square/Triangle / Q / E
Pause + Settings — Options / Esc / P
P2 join in flight — Pad 2 any button / T
Draft pick — Left / Right + Cross
Menus — D-Pad + Cross, Circle backs out

Graze enemy shots to build your Chain. Catch P crystals to power up, B for bombs, hearts to mend. Between waves, pick 1 of 3 upgrades. Clear the flagship to win!
