# E.T. the Extra-Lost-and-Found

An original unofficial tribute to the 1982 film. Adventure / stealth / environmental puzzle / flight, seen from a three-quarter third-person camera.

The slice contains a complete promise: find the visitor; offer candy; collect a talking toy, a crate-covered battery and a gated umbrella; bring flowers back with heartlight; distract and hide from patrolling searchlights; assemble and play a musical signal; cycle down the wooded road; lift over the blockade; fly through four golden rings; walk to the ship; say goodbye. Captures preserve progress and return the player to a safe checkpoint. Heartlight renews itself.

Keyboard, pointer, standard gamepad and invisible touch sticks use the same screens. Menus have directional focus navigation. Attract mode starts at 10 seconds and runs the actual route with capture immunity. Developer mode exposes four live balance sliders, safe capture, frozen patrols, refills, collision geometry, chapter jumps, and the same autopilot.

AI Hub uses the unmodified v1.4.0 client in head and ghost mode for 1–4. Standalone rooms use PeerJS WebRTC plus BroadcastChannel on the same origin. No chat or custom names.

Everything visual is code-built geometry, canvas texture, or HTML/CSS. Native AnimationMixer tracks are the base for character motion. All sounds are Web Audio oscillators and generated noise; original music starts at 10%. CDN libraries and optional fonts require network access on first load.
