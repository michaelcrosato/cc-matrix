A LITTLE DEMON KNOW-HOW

SHOOT THE FORMATION
Demons split into diving offspring. Finish every last wing to clear the night.

MAKE ROOM
Dash grants a short invulnerable burst. Pulse damages every enemy and clears their bullets. Kills recharge it.

CATCH THE DIAMONDS
Yellow gives 17 seconds of triple FORKFIRE. Cyan repairs one hull point. Each cleared night also repairs one.

BRING BACKUP
Local: second controller, or J/L move, I fire, O dash, U pulse. Downed wingmates return in seven seconds while someone survives.

CONTROLLER Stick / D-pad move · ✕ fire · ○ dash · □ pulse · Options pause

KEYBOARD / MOUSE A/D or ←/→ move · Space / click fire · Shift dash · E / right click pulse · Esc pause. Mouse moves your ship.

TOUCH Drag anywhere to steer; firing is automatic. A second finger dashes. Tap PULSE when ready.
