# Source research

Read before implementation:

- https://ballyalley.com/cart_manuals/text_manuals/incredible_wizard.txt — original manual transcription. Confirms one projectile in flight, three ordinary hunter tiers, corridor reveal, wall-free radar, escaping flying bonus, teleports, simultaneous two-player maze, and independent bonus-creature/Wizard multipliers for the following maze.
- https://ballyalley.com/cart_manuals/pdf_manuals/Incredible_Wizard%2C_The.pdf — manual scan cross-check. The rendered scan visually confirms the printed controls, scoring and radar descriptions; scan fetching was intermittently slow.
- https://vgpavilion.com/mags/1982/12/vi/conquering-the-incredible-wizard/ — December1982 Videogaming Illustrated feature. Used for lane tactics, escalating exposure, ominous/boastful voice and contemporary sound descriptions. Where the feature's multiplier wording conflicts with the manual, the manual wins. Its claims about ordinary enemies shooting on Hard and multi-kills are not used as established historical rules.
- https://archive.org/details/IncredibleWizardBallyArcadeAstrocade1982 — archive metadata fetched and a frame at40seconds inspected (`source-footage.png`). Observed dark background, rigid bright maze lines, colored compact actors, and a separate radar instrument. A still frame establishes appearance, not exact movement/AI or audio. No human listening check occurred; historical synthesis and exact AI remain unknown.

The new game uses original room layouts, anatomy, palette/material choices, writing, music, SFX and gibberish voice. Bell reveal/lure, assistance, campaign checkpoints, friendly-fire-free simultaneous co-op and the three-manifestation ending are deliberate new decisions. The source cartridge credits Tom McHugh, Scot Norris and Julie Malan; it is licensed from Bally/Midway's Wizard of Wor. Original copyrighted graphics/audio/code are not included.

Engine/Hub provenance is recorded separately. `PLAN.md` is the supplied plan. The title was chosen with Node crypto.randomInt from50 silently brainstormed candidates.
