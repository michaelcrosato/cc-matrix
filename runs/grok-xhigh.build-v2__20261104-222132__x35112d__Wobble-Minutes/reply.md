**Wobble Minutes** is a playable single-page browser game: a third-person balance comedy at Tippy Cay, inspired by Wii Fit.

- Open `Wobble Minutes-Wii Fit-grok-4.7-xhigh-20261104-222132.html` in Chrome or Edge (WebGPU; `file://` is fine).
- Lean with the left stick, WASD, or the mouse. Confirm with Cross, Space, or click. Esc pauses.
- Choose New Stay for the five-day resort, or Play Together for the 1–4 player medley.
