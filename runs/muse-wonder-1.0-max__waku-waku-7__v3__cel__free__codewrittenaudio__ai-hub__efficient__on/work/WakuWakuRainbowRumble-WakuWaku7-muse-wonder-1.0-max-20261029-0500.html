Rounds: best of 3, 60 seconds each. Bonks build your WAKU meter. At full meter it sparkles — unleash a WAKU Burst for huge damage! Blocking stops most bonks but throws grab blockers. Every fighter has a different wish, speed and special: try them all!

Arcade: climb 5 fights. Training: endless practice with a kind dummy. Hub: in an AI Hub session the game skips menus and your friends join as rivals — seat 1 fights left, seat 2 fights right, extra friends cheer-meter you!

About & Credits

Waku Waku Rainbow Rumble is an original spiritual successor to Waku Waku 7. Seven hopeful goofballs chase seven Rainbow Orbs, certain their wish is the wishiest. No art, music, characters or text from the source game are used — only its big-hearted, bonkers tournament spirit, lovingly modernized with 3D cel shading, physics bonks and online Hub brawls.
