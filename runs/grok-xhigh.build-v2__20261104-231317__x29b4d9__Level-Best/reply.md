Level Best is a browser balance game: lean a wooden peg doll through a week of island classes, alone or with up to four players.

- Open `Level Best-Wii Fit-grok-4.7-xhigh-20261104-231317.html` in a browser (file://, nothing to install).
- Press Enter or Space. Lean with a stick, WASD or arrows, or the mouse. Flail is Square or F.
- Pause opens the menu and dev sliders. Ten idle seconds on the title starts a bot demo; any input leaves it.
