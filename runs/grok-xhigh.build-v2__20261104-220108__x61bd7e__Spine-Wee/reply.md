**Spine Wee** is a single-file browser game: a five-day wellness retreat of twelve balance minigames, coached by a talking scale, playable solo or in a local class of up to four.

- Open `Spine Wee-Wii Fit-grok-4.7-xhigh-20261104-220108.html` in a browser (`file://` is fine).
- Lean with the left stick, WASD, or the mouse. Confirm with Cross, Enter, or click.
- From the title, choose New Member, Class, or Settings. After 10 seconds with no input, attract mode starts.
