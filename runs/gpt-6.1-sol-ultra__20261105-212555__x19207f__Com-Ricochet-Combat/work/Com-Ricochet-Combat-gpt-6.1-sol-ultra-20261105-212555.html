The field manual

You’re the mint machine. Land four hits to take a field. The five-field circuit adds up every hit — keep firing, even if a round gets away.

Drive. Aim. Fire.

Make the walls work.

Bank-shot rounds rebound twice and must bounce once before scoring. Guided shells bend toward your aim while you hold fire. The dotted sight shows your first bank.

Now you see me.

Invisible tanks show themselves when firing, taking a hit, or touching a wall. Your burst becomes a radar pulse. Your own tank stays faintly visible.

Take to the sky.

Jets keep moving. Steer with your movement stick or WASD; aim with the right stick or mouse. Fly off an edge and wrap to the other side. Clouds hide rivals.

Couch player 2: arrow keys to drive and aim, Enter to fire, Right Shift to burst. A second controller works too. Touch: drag left to drive, right to aim; firing is assisted.
