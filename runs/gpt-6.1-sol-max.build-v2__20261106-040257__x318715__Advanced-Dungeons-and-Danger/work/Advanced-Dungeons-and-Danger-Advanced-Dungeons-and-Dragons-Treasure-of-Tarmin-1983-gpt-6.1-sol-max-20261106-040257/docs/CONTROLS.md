# Controls

WASD/arrow keys step relative to heading. Q/E quarter-turn. Mouse: double-click the view to capture, move to look; Escape frees the cursor. With a free cursor, click objects/context actions and menus; middle-drag looks. F interacts; Enter/left click commits the chosen attack in combat. Right click/G guards. 1 chooses basic War,2 basic Spirit,3 held weapon. Tab/I pack,X swap,M map,H rest,U use held utility,V inspect,T next target,R retreat,Escape/P menu.

Standard mapping controller: left stick step, right stick look,L1/R1 quarter-turn,R2 commit,L2 guard,Cross interact/confirm,Triangle pack,Square swap,Circle back/retreat,Touchpad/L3 map,Options menu. D-pad left/right selects a combat action, D-pad up inspects, R3 changes target. Create+Options remains reserved to AIHub. PS glyphs default; Settings can show Xbox and rebind supported actions. Disconnect does not discard your expedition.

Touch: left-half drag steps; right-half drag looks; contextual prompt interacts/commits. Double-tap right side opens pack; two-finger tap opens map. Menu remains in the corner. Touch assistance selects useful channel choices; no permanent stick/button bank. The same pack/map/settings support tapping.

Combat actions are deliberate; selecting an attack does not commit it. Solo has no turn timer. In co-op the first commitment opens one host-owned8-second deadline; all living players ready resolves early. Uncommitted players guard on expiry. Settings are local and the online world remains live. Gate crossing is personal, one way; each adventurer selects their own channel balance. Descent requires both living players at the ladder and ready. A ration revives a nearby downed ally through the pack/context button; downed players do not block descent.
