# Rules and campaign implementation

The authoritative model is `src/core/model.js`. It has no renderer, DOM, GPU, socket, audio node, animation callback, or implicit regeneration. Network authority and local play submit the same commands. Rules use deterministic arithmetic; the seed and RNG state are carried in every versioned snapshot. Host monotonic time advances only the clock and cooperative planning deadlines. No exploration resource is consumed by waiting.

There are exactly six personal pack slots and two hands. Food and arrows are counters. Keys stay on the shared keyring and are never consumed. Stable item-instance IDs survive dropping, swapping a full pack, collecting, handing off, and recovering a departed player's belongings. Armor and rings leave the pack when worn. The left-hand shield contributes only to War defense.

Damage is the stated integer attack power, multiplied by the appropriate live tuning value and rounded, minus matching defense. Enemy hits can be fully absorbed; attacks against enemies always do at least one damage. Guard subtracts four from the next matching hit after armor/ring/shield. Text events record the same power, defense, guard, and final loss applied to state. Losing either personal strength downs the adventurer. Spiritual attacks never consume personal Spirit.

War strike (3 damage, adjacent) is always available. Taking either reusable rune permanently unlocks Spirit spark (3 damage, range three) for every adventurer. Reusable weapons must be in a hand; consumable throwing weapons and scrolls may be used from the pack. Bows require an arrow and a clear orthogonal corridor. Invalid range, line of sight, items, ammunition, targets, and stale round commands cost nothing.

Ordinary enemies die when either strength reaches zero. The warden is explicitly a three-seal boss: all applied channel damage contributes to persistent `damageTaken`, seals break at one-third and two-thirds of its scaled total, and the final seal breaks at the total. Channel damage remains visible in the corresponding War/Spirit pool throughout. Phase changes provide a safe preparation beat; they never refill a damaged pool. Physical pressure changes to Spirit pressure and then alternation. Boss defeat unseals the treasure; victory requires a separate ordinary interaction with the Emberheart.

Solo combat resolves a committed action immediately and then waits indefinitely. In co-op the first commitment owns one deadline, initially eight seconds. The second living participant's commitment resolves early. Missing intentions guard at deadline; duplicates and revisions cannot replace a committed intention or restart the clock. Friendly actions resolve in stable ID order before surviving enemy actions. Revalidation makes an invalidated friendly intention a resource-free guard. A slain enemy cannot deliver its scheduled blow. Telegraphs retain the selected target/channel until resolution. The brute's hammer attacks the telegraphed tile and misses if it was left. Nearby allies may share corridor cells.

One shared encounter can be active. Movement carries an epoch; triggering combat or changing floor advances it, rejecting stale movement. A distant ally can approach using tactical one-tile moves. Entering an unrelated enemy's trigger is rejected during a shared encounter. Retreat applies the stated parting strikes once and returns the party to validated safe tiles. Downed or disconnected adventurers never block a round. Nearby revival spends exactly one ration and raises each strength to at least 30% of its maximum, preserving a higher surviving value. Departure recovers hands, pack, worn equipment, food, and arrows on reachable safe stone while retaining shared keys.

Rest spends one ration and restores up to eight War and five Spirit, clamped to maxima. It is allowed during safe exploration and the boss preparation beats, and rejects waste at full strengths. Rest never occurs automatically. The wall-sight book lasts twelve movement steps; turning, inspecting, and waiting do not spend the duration. The trapped coffer explicitly reveals a wire and offers safe disarming before opening. Gate confirmation previews all three channel choices; pool loss is clamped to one. Ladders require confirmation by every standing connected adventurer at the same stair. Downed allies travel with the party.

The three authored 11×11 floors contain 44 initial entities: a lantern junction and keyed descent; flooded script passages with a ring, guarded sentinel, training, coffer, hollow secret, gate, brute and wraith; and final equipment, a mixed encounter, safe lantern, three-seal warden and treasure. The direct normal-rules verification route includes all five ordinary roles, both channels, armor/ring/shield, bow arrows, throwing and scroll consumption, full-pack exchange, reusable implements, rest, both training books, trap disarm, wall-sight/secret, gate, keyed doors, descent, boss preparation and deliberate treasure claim.

`tools/route.mjs` observes public state and uses production commands. It never changes tuning, health, position, items, clock, enemy state, or invulnerability. Its current solo route has 175 accepted commands, 92 movement steps, 38 combat rounds, eight enemy defeats, eight rests, one secret, one gate, and two boss seal changes; it ends with 38/44 War and 26/32 Spirit. These are script results, not claims about human play time.

`node tools/rules-test.mjs` prints failures only plus one count line. The 51 checks cover content, damage, survival, fallback tools, inventory, consumption, rounds/deadlines, stale commands, authority transactions, co-op revival/handoff/departure/descent, gates/traps/secrets, persistence/retry, tuning, full normal-rules solo and cooperative campaigns, completed-vault continuation, boss/treasure separation, and clean end. The cooperative pilot uses ordinary follower movement, committed guards, personal gate choices, shared descent readiness, and preparation rests. It ends with both adventurers standing. This is model-level evidence; real-peer evidence belongs to the network integration tests. The first new co-op assertion mistakenly counted the expected full-pack comparison preview as a failure and was corrected; it was a test issue, not a game defect. Later integration findings belong in the root build's issue ledger.

## Designer data

`src/content/campaign.js` exports `CONTENT`, `ITEMS`, `ENEMIES`, `FLOORS`, and `DEFAULT_TUNING`. New room geometry is row data (`#` wall and `.` floor); entities, exit destinations, encounters, names, descriptions, item powers, enemy defenses, intentions, and tutorial supplies are ordinary validated definitions. Entity IDs must stay unique. Loot uses an item kind, counter supplies, or a shared key. Chests list item kinds. Enemies may share an `encounter` ID, with a budget of at most two. Gates carry destination coordinates and numerical channel effects.

`validateContent(data)` checks structure, item/enemy numerical values, unique IDs, geometry, spawns, spawn collisions, referenced items/enemies/floors, key/lock reachability, secret paths, gate landing and forward exit proof, encounter budgets, and required direct-route supplies. `model.importContent(data)` validates first and starts a fresh visibly modified, unscored expedition. Edited content is included in its snapshots and saves as `state.content`; stock snapshots omit the redundant definitions. The UI workshop can preview/edit/import/export this same object. To ship designer changes, replace the content definition and rebuild the single HTML through the root build command.

`model.snapshot()` contains only current JSON state. `model.exportSave()` also includes the exact floor-entry checkpoint. `load` validates versions, counters, inventory references, duplicate object ownership, shared progression and rounds before replacing live state. A raw snapshot still produces a viable retry checkpoint; a full exported save retains the exact checkpoint. Retry restores floor entry with at least 70% of each maximum and three rations, keeping an exhausted death state from repeating.
