Engine Feedback
- Liked most: TSL node materials made the cel look cheap to build (toon gradient map, stepped sky, patterned ground, outline hull). Rapier's scene queries with collision groups gave reliable hit detection with little code, and viewport/scissor made split-screen easy.
- Stuck or guessed wrong: viewport Y origin (checked the source: top-left on WebGPU); Rapier hit property names (used a fallback); outline gaps on hard-edged meshes until the hull normals were welded; my own boss.body name clash with the physics body.
- Most important thing built myself: the whole game layer: rail frame and stage scripting, camera rigs, input mapping with focus-based menus, HUD, procedural audio sequencer.
- Hardest part: verifying a GPU-rendered game with no visual review; errors are asynchronous and headless WebGPU needs SwiftShader flags.
- Overhaul one thing: the implicit WebGL2 fallback in WebGPURenderer. It has to be actively disabled and the backend checked.
- Change one thing: a built-in hard-edge-safe outline pass or node, and a first-class headless test mode with clear shader and pipeline errors.
