Leave a little breathing room.

Press E or K to drop dynamite. You get six sticks. Get clear before the fuse runs out.

♡

Find every missing miner.

Fly close to a miner to send them safely topside. Rescue everyone, then follow the green extraction beacon. Finish all five assignments.

▰

A little power goes a long way.

Flying and firing use power. Cyan battery packs top you up. Your kit and hearts refill at the next assignment. Lava is a terrible shortcut.

Ⅱ

Bring a workmate.

Local co-op: player 2 uses a second controller, or the arrow keys to move and fly, N to fire, M for dynamite. Share the rescue tally.

READ THE ROCK: striped walls can be blasted. Orange rock burns. Lamps break if shot. Rafts offer a breather over lava. Tentacles are best given a wide berth.
TOUCH: left thumb drags to move and fly. Right thumb aims and fires. A nearby-threat assist helps with the rest; tap the blast button to drop dynamite.
