# Source sound research

Source: H.E.R.O. (Activision, Atari 2600, 1984).

Firsthand review describes a laser twang, a small dynamite explosion, and the propeller sound:
https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/atari2600/563243-hero/reviews/163101

Another firsthand review reports no music or title theme, with a small set of effects:
https://www.mobygames.com/game/10150/hero/user-review/2341538/

The demo synthesizes its own rotor, pitched laser, noise/low-frequency dynamite blast, and cue sounds. Its quiet ambient music and gibberish miner voices are new additions rather than reproductions of the original audio. No audio files are used.

Exact original waveform fidelity or an auditory listening comparison: unknown.
