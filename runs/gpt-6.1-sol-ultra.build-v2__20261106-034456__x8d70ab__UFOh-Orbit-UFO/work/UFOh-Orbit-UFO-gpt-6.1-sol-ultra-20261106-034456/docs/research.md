The official manual takes priority where observations differ. It does not specify exact recharge seconds or clockwise direction; those details are secondary observations. Exact source waveforms, presence/absence of continuous music, enemy timings, collision minutiae, and original missile guidance are **unknown** here. The archive's sound listing was inspected (https://www.odyssey2.info/media/sounds.php); it has no UFO! sample. The video archive and public recording search were consulted before composition; no accessible UFO! gameplay audio was auditioned. The playable game's sounds and music are wholly new.

New design additions: four authored sectors, command craft with three component phases, three hull, independent mouse/right-stick aim requests, bounded rails and gates, persistent storage, chain ownership/depth, local and Hub co-op, ready-shield rescue, score-attack wave deck, attract showcase, accessibility options, and designer tools. These are not attributed to the 1981 original. Reference images remain in `docs/references/` for research only and are excluded from the shipped HTML.
