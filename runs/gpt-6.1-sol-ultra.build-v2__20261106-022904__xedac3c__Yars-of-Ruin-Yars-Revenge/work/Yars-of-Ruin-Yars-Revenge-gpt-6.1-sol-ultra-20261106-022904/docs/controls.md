# Controls and play

Open the root HTML directly in a browser. No installation or server is needed. From the title choose Begin the siege, Normal or Assist. Three stations require one, two and three siege hits. The last hit must intercept the moving heart. Fly into cells to bite and charge. Shoot a corridor, align your vertical position, call the rail gun, and leave that lane. If every cell is gone, touch the docked green heart to refill.

Standard gamepad: left stick moves, right stick aims, R2 fires, L2 calls the cannon, Options pauses, Cross confirms and Circle returns. Settings / Controls provides PlayStation or Xbox glyphs, deadzones and rebinding. Keyboard: WASD or arrows move, mouse aims, held left click or J fires, right click or Space calls the cannon, Escape pauses. Keyboard-only aiming follows movement. Local two-keyboard fallback: WASD/J/Space and arrows/period/slash. In local co-op, a single pad belongs to P2 and keyboard/mouse to P1; two pads are assigned separately.

Touch: drag on the left half for flight, drag on the right half to aim and fire. A contextual cannon button appears when charged. Touch assists initial evasive motion when calling the cannon. The entire arena remains visible in portrait; landscape gives a larger combat area.

Online 1–2 player co-op is supplied by AI Hub. Standalone has immediate solo and local co-op, without a matchmaking service. The host simulates both scouts; departing teammates do not strand a round. Session menus leave the online world running.
