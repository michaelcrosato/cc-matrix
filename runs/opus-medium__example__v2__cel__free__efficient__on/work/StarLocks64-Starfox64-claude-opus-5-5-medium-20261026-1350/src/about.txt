ENGINE FEEDBACK
- Liked most: TSL node materials — the toon ramp, ink outlines, sky, cel fireballs and procedural ground are each a few lines of JS with no GLSL/WGSL strings. Rapier's query API (castRay with group filters and a predicate) made weapons trivially robust against fast projectiles.
- Stuck / guessed wrong: WebGPURenderer.setViewport is documented as lower-left origin, but the WebGPU backend passes coordinates straight through (top-left), so split-screen layout had to be verified in source. Rapier 0.19 renamed hit.toi to hit.timeOfImpact; handled both.
- Most important thing built myself: the rail framework (curved corridor frame lookup, rail-relative enemy path functions, streamed scenery chunks, data-driven event timelines with route branching) plus the unified Pad model so AI, attract mode and 1–4 humans all fly the same Ship code.
- Hardest part: getting gap-free outlines on hard-edged primitives at any non-uniform scale (solved with merged smooth-normal hull geometry and modelScale-compensated offsets), and split-screen with per-viewport shadow focus.
- Remove/overhaul: the split between three.js "core" and "webgpu" builds and their import-map aliases (three vs three/webgpu vs three/tsl) is easy to get wrong for addons.
- Change one thing: built-in per-object outline/ink support (or a documented ToonOutlinePass that works with scissored split-screen viewports).

PROMPT FEEDBACK
- Liked most: very clear deliverable spec (file naming, single-file bundle, required sections) and the explicit permission to ignore performance, which let the scope go to content.
- Confusing: "4-player multiplayer" could mean co-op or versus — both were implemented (1–4 player split-screen co-op Mission Mode, 2–4 player split-screen Versus with CPU fill). "Write unknown for anything you can't confirm" vs required usage estimates — usage is reported as unknown.
- Hardest: fitting a full Starfox-style vertical slice (rail stage with branching route, mid-boss, boss, all-range stage with rival squadron and capital ship, versus, attract mode, comms, menus) into one coherent codebase without visual review.
- One test I'd run: a real-GPU playthrough with a physical DualSense on Chrome/Safari to tune feel, readability and difficulty, plus a frame-time capture for the 4-player split-screen case.

HOW TO PLAY
Steer with the left stick (WASD). Tap ✕ (Space/J/click) to fire; hold to charge a homing lock-on shot. ○ (K) bomb. R2 (Shift) boost, L2 (Ctrl/C) brake. Double-tap L1/R1 (Q/E) to barrel roll and deflect shots; hold to bank. △ (F) somersault. □ (R) U-turn in all-range mode. OPTIONS (Esc/P) pause. CREATE (V) camera.
Fly through all six golden arches on Verdalis to unlock the secret canyon route. Collect 3 gold rings to extend your shield. Laser items upgrade single → twin → hyper lasers and repair broken wings. Save your wingmen when they call for help!
