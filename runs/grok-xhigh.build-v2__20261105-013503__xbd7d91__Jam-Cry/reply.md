Jam Cry is a self-contained first-person survival shooter in one HTML page: jamming guns, wind-driven fire, fever, vehicles, contracts, companions, and three endings.

- Open `Jam Cry-Far Cry 2-grok-4.7-xhigh-20261105-013503.html` in Chrome or Edge. It runs from `file://` and needs WebGPU.
- Confirm with Enter or Space. Move with WASD and the mouse, or a standard gamepad. Esc pauses; Dev mode is on that menu.
