Craft
Three.js R180 WebGPU only, Rapier 0.19.3 physics, Gamepad API, AI Hub Session Protocol v1. Original cel-shaded fantasy art and procedural animation. Synthesized music and effects; no voice. AI Hub parallel multiplayer when launched through the hub; otherwise offline.

Testing feedback
__TEST_FEEDBACK__

Engine feedback
Three.js made an expressive world approachable. WebGPU compatibility and Rapier character movement required care. The quest, combat, interface and multiplayer integration were built for this demo. WebGPU tooling and device support were the hardest parts; clearer renderer diagnostics would help. A better built-in character controller workflow would be welcome.

Prompt feedback
The controller-first focus and vertical-slice ambition were valuable. The scope of a full successor and designer tooling in one HTML file was open-ended. Balancing a complete game loop with the technical constraints was hardest. A real PS5 controller and Safari-on-iPhone playtest would be valuable.
