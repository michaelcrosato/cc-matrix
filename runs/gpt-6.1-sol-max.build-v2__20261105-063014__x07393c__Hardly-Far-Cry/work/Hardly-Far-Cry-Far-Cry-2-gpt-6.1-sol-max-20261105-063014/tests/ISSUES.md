# Issues caught by testing

Each numbered entry records a distinct defect discovered by a test, its severity, and its resolution.
1. **Critical, fixed:** Custom cage id collided with a built-in model during module import; the game catalog now replaces same-id library entries explicitly.
2. **Critical, fixed:** Bottle-tree placement supplied an acacia-only palette slot and prevented world setup. Tree variants now receive their declared slots.
3. **Critical, fixed:** Escape opened and immediately dismissed the pause menu in one update. New screens now wait until the next update to consume menu input.
4. **Noncritical, fixed:** Fuel tanks stood on fuel-free depot dirt, so grass ignition could not reach them. Tank cells now carry building fuel and participate in the same fire grid.
5. **Noncritical, fixed:** Blast damage reached soldiers and props but omitted vehicle bodies. Explosions now damage nearby vehicles, including the payroll convoy.
6. **Critical, fixed:** Ray exclusion was passed as the fourth parameter, which is the ignore-tags list; physics updates made shots and LOS hit the shooter's capsule. All gameplay rays now pass an empty tag list and the body as the fifth parameter.
7. **Critical, fixed:** Loading every campaign actor and settlement for Cash Run exceeded the hub's 20-second ready window under multiple software-rendered copies. A session now loads the designed mine/post round content and its 23 soldiers.
8. **Noncritical, fixed:** Equipping another copy of the rifle reset the tutorial's fifth-shot counter. The tutorial now counts rifle shots across the contract, independently of gun instances.
9. **Noncritical, fixed:** Non-pause menus accidentally skipped simulation ticks while leaving the clock running. Fire, soldiers, vehicles and the body now continue during physical interactions and story choices.
10. **Noncritical, fixed:** The mobile touch test aimed using full-window coordinates despite the engine's centered canvas. It now maps UI rows through the actual canvas bounds; small portrait HUD spacing was also tightened.
11. **Noncritical, fixed:** Screenshot review found the ammo count touching the weapon label, and a subtitle panel reserved two lines but rendered with a different wrap width. HUD spacing and subtitle wrapping now agree.
12. **Noncritical, fixed:** Review of the map-at-the-wheel screenshot exposed a misleading exit prompt and a left-stick cursor/driving conflict. Map prompts now take priority, the right stick/mouse drives its cursor, and the left stick remains available for movement and driving.
13. **Noncritical, fixed:** Controller-flow review found the intro missing its native map prompt and Cross skip, and the wounded buddy's Triangle finish binding missing. These now follow the displayed native controls.
14. **Noncritical, fixed:** The alternate-plan menu existed but had no timed radio entry in normal play. Living buddies now call after a contract starts and offer the working accept/decline screen; a systems test covers the normal route.
15. **Noncritical, fixed:** Reset review found scorched tree materials, fallen actor poses and collider registration surviving a new-world reset. Reset now restores these, and attract resets its world at each loop; tests cover restoration and a full repeating loop.
16. **Noncritical, fixed:** Leaving the story train retained its mounted weapon and the old foot-controller position. Train completion now puts the capsule at the arrival point and restores the carried rifle; new Cash Runs also reset their loadout instead of inheriting campaign equipment.
17. **Noncritical, fixed:** The multiplayer AI bubble used only the local view position, leaving distant clients and dedicated-server mine guards inactive. Host simulation now includes the union of all player bubbles and paths toward the selected player.
18. **Critical, fixed:** Opening the cage or collecting a mission object before its sequential step could leave the later contract waiting for an unavailable interaction. Persistent consumed-object state now satisfies those steps, stale events are cleared on start/load, and story revelations are gated to their intended mission. Regression tests exercise the out-of-order cage and payroll routes.
19. **Noncritical, fixed:** The screenshot script returned the entire ready Engine through Playwright, serializing its large scene/geometry graph and causing avoidable memory spikes. It now awaits readiness and returns only a boolean.
20. **Noncritical, fixed:** Control checks found keyboard F toggled the gun seat without equipping its gun, R3 used the wrong driving action, and pad sprint required holding the stick click. Native mount/chase/sprint mappings now work, leaving a mount restores the carried gun, and crouched/walking movement stays quiet.
