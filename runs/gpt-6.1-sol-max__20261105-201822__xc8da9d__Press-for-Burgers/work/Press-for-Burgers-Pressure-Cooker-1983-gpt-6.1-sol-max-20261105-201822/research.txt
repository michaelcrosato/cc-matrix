SOURCE RESEARCH — PRESSURE COOKER (1983)

Primary gameplay source: Activision’s Short-Order Sam’s Famous Fast-Food Cookbook,
transcribed at https://atariage.com/manual_html_page.php?SoftwareID=1222
Visual reference: Atari screenshot at
https://www.atarimania.com/2600/screens/pressure_cooker.gif
Sound/context: contemporary descriptions and the manual’s continuous/between-wave music setting.
Actual original waveform analysis: unknown.

The source is an action kitchen game. A chef catches flying tomato, onion, lettuce
and cheese, assembles exact recipes on an always-moving belt, catches the top bun,
and visits a wrapping room with three color-coded chutes. Unwanted toppings can be
bounced back. Food waste, duplicate toppings, misdeliveries and lost burgers lower
performance; zero performance ends the game. Waves get faster. Eight game variations
change difficulty and alternating-player setup; they are not eight different maps.
There are no conventional enemy characters: the machinery, timing and waste are
the adversaries. The screen uses a bright, simplified chef, a fiery oven, a gray
conveyor and a compact order board. The original has a cheerful electronic jingle.

INTERPRETATION
Press for Burgers is an original fixed-oblique 3D arcade kitchen, with a warm diner
palette, toon materials and ink outlines. One continuous kitchen makes assembly
and wrapping legible together. Three authored services introduce toppings and a
steam grate; dash, a rechargeable brake, streak rushes and co-op are new systems.
No original source game art, music, code or ROM is included in the deliverable.
