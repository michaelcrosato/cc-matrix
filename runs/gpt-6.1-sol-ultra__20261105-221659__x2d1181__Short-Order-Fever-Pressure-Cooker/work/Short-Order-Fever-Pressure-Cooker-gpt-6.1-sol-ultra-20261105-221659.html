Mouse: click the floor to move, ingredients to catch, hatches to deliver.
Touch: drag anywhere on the left to move. Correct catches and deliveries are assisted. Tap a ticket to switch.

Serve to cool the pressure and recharge the bell. Missed ingredients, expired tickets and wrong catches heat things up. Later shifts add more layers and a steaming floor vent. Don’t let pressure hit 100% or the shift clock run out.

ABOUT & CREDITS.

An original, affectionate arcade cooking game inspired by the ingredient conveyor, layered orders and cheerful pressure of Pressure Cooker (1983). Procedural art, animation, music, sound and gibberish voices. Built for a controller on the couch.
