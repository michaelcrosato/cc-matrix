- Open `A Cry Too Far-Far Cry 2-grok-4.7-xhigh-20261105-013917.html` in Chrome or another WebGPU browser. `file://` works.
- On the title screen, start New Game. Use WASD and the mouse, or a gamepad. Esc or Options pauses, and Dev Mode is in that menu.
- Press M to hold the map. Cash Run on the main menu is solo, host or join on this computer, or AI Hub online.
