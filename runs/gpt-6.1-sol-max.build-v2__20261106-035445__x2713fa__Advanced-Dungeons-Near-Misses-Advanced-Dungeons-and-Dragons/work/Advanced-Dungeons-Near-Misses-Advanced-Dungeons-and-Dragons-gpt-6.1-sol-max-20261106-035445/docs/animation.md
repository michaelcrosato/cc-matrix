# Native art and animation

`src/render/art.js` extends the full pinned engine through its public `Humanoid.pose`, `Humanoid.weapon`, `SHAPES`, `r.actor`, `r.shape`, and pixel primitives. It changes no engine source. The ranger is the native chibi humanoid with an original tunic palette, trail band, carried quiver, and ash bow. Creature anatomy is an original local-joint grammar of shaded balls, capsules, and membranes rendered through the same native character view and painter queue.

Call `NM.Art.ranger(game, actor)`, `creature(game, actor)`, or `prop(game, kind, x, y, options)` once after floor and wall submission. Each function queues its own draw and shadow. Callers must omit every hidden creature before invoking it; the module does not have access to gameplay fog. The root anchor is always the actor's ground position. No draw function changes its actor, advances simulation, schedules sound, or creates a projectile. The module caches presentation rigs by game and actor ID and sets their gait, held pose, expression, and clock directly from the simulation state. `Art.clear(game)` releases a game's cached rigs.

The ranger bow registers a native weapon draw hook. The hook builds the bow, string, and nocked shaft from the weapon-hand attachment and its local `Sp` transform. `nm-bow` sets the right grip to `[7.5,0,15]`; the left draw hand pulls back up to five units. `Art.bowOrigin(actor)` reports the same grip in world units at any aim angle. A projectile leaves the actual bow, including while the ranger moves. The anticipated shot and recovery use `actor.draw` and `actor.recovery`; run uses accumulated `walk` and velocity; focus lowers the gait and uses a cupped-ear pose; quickstep leans the body; hurt, downed, revival kneel, pickup, climbing, and celebration use the native skeleton's state-driven solve.

Rat legs alternate with a long pink balancing tail; ears, muzzle, and whiskers form its low silhouette. Snake coils rise during anticipation and extend into a lunge. Bat membranes fold while dormant and flex through flight and attack. Spider legs articulate in four opposing pairs and show a carried shaft. Ooze is a cluster of translucent-colored lobes with moving internal bubbles. The ground drake carries a low spine, horns, heavy legs, and a long flexible tail. Guardians enlarge that rig with jointed membranes, wing spars, loaded anticipation, swoop poses, and three physical back plates that disappear as wounds accumulate. The warm and cold guardian palettes, breathing glows, and distinct wing motion accompany their simulation-specific attacks. Defeated creatures subside into short pale dispersal particles; their state clock controls the sequence.

Tool, boat, key, guarded ladder, supply quiver, bank stone, trail marker, and circlet half are original native shape data. Generic rocks, crystals, trees, bushes, barrels, and chests use the engine library. No raster files, imported illustration packs, downloaded motion clips, or sound assets are used.

`tests/art.mjs` exercises every creature through seven states, ranger diagonal/left/right aim through six traversal poses, bow-origin geometry, prop definitions, immutable actor data, and the engine's headless draw error ledger. It supplies signal-level correctness; browser screenshots remain necessary to judge scale, occlusion, and tell readability.
