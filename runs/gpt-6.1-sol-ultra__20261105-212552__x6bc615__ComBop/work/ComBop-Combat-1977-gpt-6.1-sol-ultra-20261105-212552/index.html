Field guide / 4 useful things

Aim. Bop. Repeat.

01

A wall is an opportunity.

Shells bounce twice. Angle a shot around cover. The dotted guide shows the way. Three hits wreck a tank; three wrecks win the bout.

02

Save a little burst.

Burst to dodge or slip through a gap. It recharges in three seconds. Three shell slots refill automatically. Your own bounces can hurt you!

03

Things get peculiar.

Ghost shift: tanks fade until they fire or get hit. Double time: collect the lightning for rapid fire. Green crosses repair armour.

04

Make a little room.

Gold crates break. Red fuel cans explode. At zero time, the hot zone closes in: keep moving. Complete three bouts to take the circuit.
