Mint Dial is a self-contained browser game: a seven-day fitness island where you keep a balance dot in the ring.

- Open `Mint Dial-Wii Fit-grok-4.7-xhigh-20261104-221607.html` in Chromium with WebGPU (`file://` works).
- Use the stick, WASD or arrows (Shift halves the lean), or the mouse to lean; R2, Space, or click to breathe; L1/R1, F/J, or mouse buttons to step.
- Confirm to play the week. Pause opens settings and dev sliders. Up to four play Family Hour on one screen.
