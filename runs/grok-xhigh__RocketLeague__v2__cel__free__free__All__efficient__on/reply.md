Arena Afterburn is a single-file, cel-shaded car-soccer game in the browser, with local split-screen and online play for up to four players.

- In this folder, run `python3 -m http.server 8080`.
- Open `http://localhost:8080/Arena%20Afterburn-Rocket%20League-grok-4.7-xhigh-20261028-2106.html` in Chrome or Edge with WebGPU. There is no WebGL fallback.
- Use a controller or keyboard. From the menu, start a solo or split-screen match, or host and join online by pasting SDP. After 10 seconds with no input, an AI attract demo starts.
