# Star Ralliers
Original space combat / light strategy vertical slice, inspired by Atari Star Raiders (1982, especially the Atari 5200 release).

First person cockpit, optional aft scanner, 5×4 strategic chart. Eleven hostile craft across three fleets: fast Splinters, heavy Lancers, shielded Crown flagship. Two threatened relay bases, energy-powered twin photon cannons, target lock, shield switching, boost, emergency pulse, engine damage, precision hyperwarp, docking repair shuttle. Clear the invasion and return to either surviving base. Mission ratings and replay.

Ink silhouettes, hard light bands, turquoise / tangerine palette, low-poly model kits. No image or audio files. Three.js r180 WebGPURenderer with automatic WebGL2 fallback; Rapier 0.19.3 handles projectile collisions, ship movement and debris. Three AnimationMixer clips animate ship wings, station assemblies, docking and warp.

Controller, mouse / keyboard, touch assist. Pause offers settings and live development tools. Ten-second title attract replay. AI Hub ghost protocol for 1–4 independent pilots with visible wing ghosts and score competition. Standalone room codes: BroadcastChannel same origin and PeerJS / WebRTC remote transport.

Research: official Atari 5200 manual archived on AtariAge; Atari 2600 manual; original manuals cover chart, twin photons, three enemy archetypes, shield / energy economy, manual warp steering, base siege, repair shuttle and ranking. Three.js r180 official toon example, Rapier official documentation and pinned package; AI Hub protocol v1 / session client 1.4.0.
