# Original native art and animation

Turtle Shell Service uses the supplied my-3D2dge v0.89.1 pixel renderer throughout. No image files, sprite recordings or external art service are used. `src/art.js` defines bespoke overhead adult turtles, hitchhiking hatchlings, four communicative beetle states, a brass refill cabinet, question-mark hotel doors, symbol-coded home porches, and the miniature hotel title scene. Ordinary benches/bookcases use the engine's core shape library when authored. Every primitive is drawn through E.px, native fonts, E.TileMap, r.actor/r.queue/r.decal or native particles and lighting.

The custom body rigs retain the native E.Blob continuous spring/look/walk clock rather than frame strips. Velocity drives separate feet, four-way heading eases through turns, the shell breathes/blinks, pickup climbs onto the back, a planted trap flexes the body, delivery creates a hop-down/reunion, hurt retracts the head and feet, and invulnerability supplies a steady ring. Seat two has a striped amber shell, blue scarf and luggage tag. Beetles have relaxed/alert antennae, spots/stripes/angular chevrons, halted legs plus orbiting stars when stunned, and a recovery anticipation mark. All cosmetic clocks and springs have no authority over simulation.

The title hotel is an original three-quarter model painted with native pixel primitives. Its lift lamps foreshadow the five-floor ascent. The fixed overhead gameplay camera retains consistent directions; a rooftop airship carries the rescued family away in the celebration. Native material callbacks paint original hotel wallpaper, with blue ceramic laundry, mossy conservatory and garden palettes from floor data. Scenery specified on a corridor is safely staged on the nearest adjacent blocked cell, keeping traversable lanes readable.

Research viewed the 1983 US manual's parent-and-passenger illustration and source screenshots saved in docs. Designs, palettes, hotel, patterns and animations are original reinterpretations; no source sprite/art was copied.

Validation: `tools/art-check.py` opens a file:// preview in installed headless Chromium, captures one 1920x1080 gameplay screenshot and title composition, and reports browser page errors. It is a visual working tool; final export verification belongs to the main verifier. Screenshots establish composition and readable silhouettes, not a real TV, controller or human listening test.

Developer inspection is rendered by native primitives and the same E.Grid used by the simulation. Graph edges follow native neighbours/walkability; yellow cardinal sight and red corner routes use native sees/path queries. Contact circles expose the actual actor, home, supply, bomb-trigger and bomb-effect radii. The reveal-data flag annotates hidden rooms with HATCH/BUG and delivered/carried status, without changing their contents. Snapshot-only clients construct a native fallback graph from the same authored rows. `tests/art.test.mjs` checks all five floors, reveal confidentiality by default, snapshot drawing, and absence of rules mutations.
