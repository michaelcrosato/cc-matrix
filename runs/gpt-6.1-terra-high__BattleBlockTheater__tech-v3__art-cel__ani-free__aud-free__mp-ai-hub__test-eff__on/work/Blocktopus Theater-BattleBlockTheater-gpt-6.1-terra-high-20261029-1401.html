How to play

Move with left stick / D-pad or arrows / A,D. Jump with ✕ / Space. Push crates by walking into them. Collect enough gems, grab the key, and reach the door. Springs bounce you; switches make bridges. Avoid spikes and angry stagehands. Checkpoints save your progress. Pause with Options / Esc.
