**Hands of Harbor: Second Opinion** is a playable single-file browser surgery game set in a harbour hospital.

- Open `Hands of Harbor: Second Opinion-Trauma Center: Second Opinion-grok-4.7-xhigh-20261104-224015.html` in Chrome or Edge (WebGPU; `file://` is fine).
- Aim with the right stick or mouse, operate with R2 or left click, and pick tools with the left stick or keys 1–8.
- On the title, 10 seconds of idle starts attract mode; Options or Esc pauses into settings and Dev Mode.
