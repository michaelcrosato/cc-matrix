    9 Signature Weapons

    • [1] War Blade & Talon: Twin wrist blades for close-quarters decapitation.
    • [2] Tek Compound Bow: Toggle Standard or Explosive Tek Arrows with [E / R1] and Sniper Scope with [RMB / L2].
    • [3] Mag-60 Burst Pistol: 3-round high-velocity armor-piercing burst sidearm.
    • [4] The Shredder: Fires 6 energy flechettes that ricochet up to 3 times off cavern walls.
    • [5] Endtrail Plasma Rifle: Long-range emerald plasma rifle with sniper scope.
    • [6] Firestorm Cannon: 6-barrel rotary gatling plasma repeater.
    • [7] Sunfire Pod: Blinding solar grenade that stuns foes and deals 3.2x damage to subterranean Blind Ones.
    • [8] The Cerebral Bore: Locks onto enemy brainwaves, latches onto their head, drills out cerebral fluid for 2.2s, and detonates the cranium!
    • [9] The Nuke (Chrono-Grains): Collect the 3 Nuke Parts across the zones to unleash a cataclysmic singularity shockwave!

    Zones & Mission Mechanics

    • Port of Adia: Destroy the 3 purple Primagen Grain-Caches, rescue the 3 Captive Sun-Seedlings, and purge the central Energy Totem.
    • River of Souls: Dive underwater (watch your Oxygen meter!) to recover Sunken Key #1 & Nuke Part #1, and climb the mossy Vine Wall for Key #2.
    • Temple of the Wind: Claim the Sacred Feather Talisman to soar on emerald Wind Totem Jump Pads, and battle Blind Ones & Purr-Linn Juggernauts.
    • Lightship Boss Core: Step through Adon's Warp Portal or open the Soul-Gate to slay The Primagen's Harbinger!
