# Authoritative rules

- The simulation is independent of DOM/render/audio/network. One host advances fixed 1/120-second ticks. Input is keyed by real seat ID. Directions are 0 north, 1 east, 2 south, 3 west.
- All movement, pathfinding and tunnel wrapping share the board graph. Actors travel continuously between cell centres; turns buffer for 150 ms, held intent refreshes it, and reversing swaps endpoints without changing position. On release, the established arcade direction continues until a wall.
- Tunnels are explicit paired edges. Seam encounters use unwrapped relative motion. Encounter order is control/timers, motion, food (stable ID order), power activation, predators, then stage decisions. A final bite wins before a simultaneous predator collision can consume a life.
- Twelve uniquely identified foods (eight ordinary, four bright) follow committed non-reversing routes. Route decisions have small seeded variation; at intersections they favour distance from players but never instantly reverse. A short turn anticipation makes route changes legible. Survivor speed grows monotonically from 0.62× to 1× nominal hero speed. Intersections and crosslinks enable interception.
- Three distinct predators release in sequence. Follower targets a nearest hero; interceptor targets a forward route; keeper patrols central approaches unless a hero is close. Decisions occur at cell centres after bounded reaction intervals. Frightened predators retreat. Eaten predators are harmless fast returners; only returners may use the pen's harmless return route regardless of opening. They recharge before release.
- Bright food refreshes power to six seconds (never adds indefinitely) and resets the catch chain. Catch rewards are 5, 10, then 20; power expiration resets it. Last 1.5 seconds is recovery warning with a distinct state.
- The pen has one graph opening, rotates every 2.6 seconds and warns for its final 0.5 seconds. Rotation defers while an actor occupies any crossing edge. Actors wholly inside wait safely. All other required routes remain connected. The cadence is read from live configuration.
- Five shared lives, one per readable hit. Only the affected hero respawns, with 1.5 seconds invulnerability; team damage grace is 1.2 seconds. Collected food persists. A clear restores one life up to five. Zero lives ends the run.
- Reveal chirp affects visibility only, with a four-second cooldown. Invisible boards reveal initially, at each board’s authored reveal period and briefly upon wall collision. Touch may trigger the same ordinary reveal action automatically.
- Campaign has six real stage transitions. Custom/remix finish after one board. Clears pause action for 2.4 seconds; configuration, score, lives and statistics persist while temporary power/input state resets. Events have unique IDs and bounded retention for deduplicated presentation.
- Tuning changes apply at the next fixed tick. Cheats are absent by default; an explicitly assisted/attract configuration may grant invulnerability but cannot be used as normal completion evidence.
