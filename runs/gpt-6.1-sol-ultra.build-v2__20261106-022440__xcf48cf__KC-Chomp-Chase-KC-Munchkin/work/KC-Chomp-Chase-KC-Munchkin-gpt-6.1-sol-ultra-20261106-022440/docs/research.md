# Source evidence ledger

Research performed 2026-11-06 before content authoring. Historical facts below are separated from this game's inventions. No original game's artwork or audio is embedded in the game.

| [Original US rules booklet, ©1981](https://odyssey2.info/library/manuals/us_kcmunchkin/K.C.%20Munchkin%20(USA).pdf), printed pages 2–14 | One controllable Munchkin, three Munchers, twelve moving Munchies, cardinal travel, edge wrapping, a rotating center, edible-enemy reversal and a 1/3/5/10/20 scoring ladder. Clearing food continues the run at increased difficulty. Four standard mazes, random mazes, movement-triggered invisible walls with collision reveal, and a programmable wall editor are documented. The game's six original mazes/seeded layout, two-player simultaneous cooperative rules, three distinct predator personalities, safe pen rotation, reveal chirp, controller-first workshop, procedural score and original characters are adaptations. The historical identity comes from food that runs away, escalating last-target interception, a turning center, wrapping paths, and brief reversals of hunter and prey.
