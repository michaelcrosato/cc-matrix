How to Play

Follow the gold objective marker. Destroy 3 Seed Pods, survive the waves, defeat the Seed Tyrant, enter the portal.
Weapons: 1 Tek Bow (hold to charge) · 2 Scattergun · 3 Plasma Lance · 4 Cerebral Seeker (homing).
Pick up leaves (health), shards (armor), quivers (ammo). Crates break. Enemies: Raptors bite, Mantids spit, Brutes slam.
Attract mode starts after 10s idle on title. Any input exits it.
