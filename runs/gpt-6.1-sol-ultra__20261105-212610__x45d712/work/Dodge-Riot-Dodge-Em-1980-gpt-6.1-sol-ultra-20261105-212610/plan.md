# Dodge Riot implementation plan

Original arcade lane chase inspired by Dodge Em (1980), from native knowledge only.
Three rounds, four rounded rectangular lanes with four interchange gates, collectible fare dots,
oncoming rivals, rechargeable boost, brake, shields, near-miss combo, shared-life co-op.
Orthographic 3D cel/ink scene, native Three animation mixer for cars and scenery.
Single HTML bundle with pinned external Three.js 0.180.0 / Rapier 0.19.3 dependencies.
Keyboard, standard gamepads, assisted invisible touch controls, pause/settings/dev/about.
Attract demonstration after ten seconds. Hub shared-world host-authoritative snapshots plus
standalone local co-op and direct WebRTC rooms. Procedural Web Audio only.
Verify simulation, controls, complete run, UI, touch, audio, networking, and visuals.
