# Research and provenance

The source brief is interpreted as Bally's 1979 Astrocade Space Invaders, subsequently Astro Battle, rather than Sidam's similarly named arcade game. This is the plan's working interpretation, not a new release-history claim. The renamed manual itself bears a 1981 copyright.

Examined sources:

- [Bally Alley HTML manual](https://ballyalley.com/cart_manuals/html_manuals/astro_battle.htm), read in full. It establishes horizontal base movement, upward fire, four difficulty settings, damage to bunkers by both sides, accelerating survivors, lower subsequent formations, a harmless bonus parent ship, row-based 10/20/30/40 scores, 100/200/300 parent bonuses, three starting bases, one extra at 1,000 and an ending after six formations.
- [Five-page manual scan](https://ballyalley.com/cart_manuals/pdf_manuals/Astro_Battle.pdf), saved as `manual.pdf`. Rasterized and inspected the playing-instructions page, scan page 3. The web screenshot endpoint could not return the scan; local rasterization supplied the visual evidence. Text extraction on these scans is unavailable because the pages are images.
- [October 1982 comparison article](https://ballyalley.com/articles_and_news/Video_Games_October_1982.pdf), saved as `comparison.pdf`. Inspected scan page 4 / printed page 66. It describes colorful crustacean-like marchers, the unusual marching beat, saucer event and the finite sixth-attack ending. No historical audio listening was performed.
- [Cartridge-video archive](https://ballyalley.com/astrocade_videos/cartridge_videos/cartridge_videos.html), including its Astro Battle link to [Nice and Games, LSYrGZm0I9Q](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LSYrGZm0I9Q). Metadata identifies a 353-second review published January 29, 2010. Direct MP4 download was denied with HTTP 403. A short HLS clip requested around 80-96 seconds succeeded; see `source-clip.mp4` and `source-filmstrip.png`. Examined eight frames spaced two seconds apart: the cartridge and difficulty menus, horizontal movement of colored four-row troops, alternating silhouettes, a low yellow cannon, four orange arch-shaped bunkers and their visible erosion. The short clip is silent video, so exact historical sound events and audio cadence remain unknown. No ROM or commercial asset extraction was used.

The game uses original silhouettes and authored four-row/eight-column arrangements; that column count is a new layout, not a claim about the source. Simultaneous/online co-op, deterministic salvage and repair, specialist armour/bombardiers, new locations, the hostile flagship and all writing are original additions. The bonus saucer remains harmless and is visually distinct from the flagship. No fuel/docking loop or diving enemies were imported.

The arcade helper alternates two-player turns and was not used for simultaneous co-op. Its endpoint bullet helpers were not used for damage.
