The Second Opinion Clinic is a playable single-file browser game: timed surgery, a hospital story, and co-op for up to four players.

- Open `The-Second-Opinion-Clinic-Trauma-Center-Second-Opinion-grok-4.7-xhigh-20261104-224040.html` in a browser with WebGPU.
- Press Enter, click, or Cross on the title, then choose On Call for the story or Consult for local co-op.
- Aim with the mouse, WASD, or the left stick; operate with left click, J, or R2. Esc or Options pauses into Dev Mode.
