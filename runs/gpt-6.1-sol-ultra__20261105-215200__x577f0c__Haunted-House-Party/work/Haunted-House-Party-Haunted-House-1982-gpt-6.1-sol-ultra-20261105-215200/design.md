# Haunted House Party

Original adventure inspired by Atari's Haunted House (1982). Isometric survival horror / puzzle adventure. Four floors, six rooms each. One hand forces a choice between master key, protective scepter, and the assembling urn. Nine shared nerves, matches, dashes, door locks, staircase tunes and three enemy behaviors. Returning the completed urn to the foyer wins. All floors are designed for reusable room data and continuous Rapier collision. AI demonstration uses the same state and a navigation grid.

Art: blue-green moonlight, warm amber matchlight, bone-white eyes, ink silhouettes, woodcut patterns and burgundy rugs. Native Three.js mixers animate cloth, floating ghosts, spider legs and bat wings. Procedural waltz, creaks, wind, gibberish greetings. Controller-first large menus, focus navigation and responsive glyphs. Touch automatic light and context interaction, drag anywhere to move.

Source observations: four floors of six rooms; three urn fragments; unlimited counted matches; nine lives; a single held object; key, protective scepter; slow spiders, fast bat and wall-passing ghost; enemies and wind extinguish matches; eyes in darkness, flat room shapes; footsteps, slammed doors, thunder, ascending/descending stair phrases. This game adds isometric mansion detail, a dodge, readable enemy warnings, a mapped journal and shared-world co-op.
