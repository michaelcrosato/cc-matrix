# Source research

Source: Kaboom!, Activision, Atari 2600, 1981; designer Larry Kaplan.

- Original instruction manual: https://www.atariage.com/manual_html_page.php?SoftwareLabelID=257
- Game reference and screenshots: https://www.atariage.com/software_page.php?SoftwareLabelID=257
- Original scanned manual: https://www.gamingalexandria.com/highquality/atari2600/Kaboom%20%28Atari%202600%29/Kaboom%21%20-%20Manual.pdf

Confirmed: paddle-controlled horizontal movement of three stacked water buckets; a single prison-striped Mad Bomber crossing the upper edge; monochrome round bombs; splash/catch sounds, percussive explosions and an expressive bomber. Eight successive bomb groups increase speed and count; the last repeats. A miss detonates the field and removes a bucket. Three lost buckets ends play. Each 1,000 points replaces a missing bucket. Expert mode halves bucket width. Original two-player play alternates turns. This game's simultaneous co-op, golden bombs, gusts, splash ability, authored two-minute show and endless option are original variations.

The research establishes the sound's general catch/explosion vocabulary, not its exact original waveforms or pitches. No original recording was copied or used. Sound descriptions were cross-checked against https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/atari2600/584825-kaboom/reviews/10245 (fuse sizzle, rising-pitch catch effects, explosions and no music in the original). Exact original sound synthesis parameters: unknown.

Engine documentation checked: Three.js r180 WebGPURenderer source and MeshToonNodeMaterial source; official Rapier JavaScript setup and compatibility package documentation. AI Hub README, session client, retrofit guide and harness were read from the linked repository; the official session client is preserved unmodified.
