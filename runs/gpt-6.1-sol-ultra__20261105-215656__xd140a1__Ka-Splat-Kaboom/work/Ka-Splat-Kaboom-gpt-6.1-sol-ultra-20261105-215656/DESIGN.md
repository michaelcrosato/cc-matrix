# Ka-Splat!
A controller-first arcade catching game inspired by Kaboom! (1981). Fixed front-facing orthographic 2.5D rooftop diorama. Eight escalating patterns culminate in a complete show; endless mode extends replay. Three stacked water buckets, miss-clears-field pressure, score-based repairs and a recharging splash rescue preserve the source's rhythm. Gold bombs, gusts, feints, co-op and procedural cartoon presentation are original additions.

Native Three.js AnimationMixer clips drive idle and bomb-toss poses; Rapier dynamic bodies drive drops. Everything, including art, sound and voices, is generated in code. Music starts at 10 percent. All modules and dependencies are bundled into one root HTML release. No copied game assets.

Controls: left stick / D-pad / A D / arrows or mouse horizontal movement; Cross / Space / click splash; Options / Escape pause. Two local controllers or P2 J L and I; touch drag plus second touch/tap splash. Full menu focus works with pad, keyboard and pointer. Pause includes live developer balance tools.
