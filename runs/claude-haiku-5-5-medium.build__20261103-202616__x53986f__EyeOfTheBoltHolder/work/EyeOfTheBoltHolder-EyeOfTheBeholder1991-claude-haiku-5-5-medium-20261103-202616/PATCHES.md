# Engine patches

None. The engine files in `engine/` are the v0.85.2 copies from `dist/handoff/my-3d2dge.js` and `dist/my-3d2dge-3d.js`,
unmodified. The things the plan asked to patch (button symbols, pause items as functions, audio buses, beards, touch
buttons) were worked around in the game: the pause items are an array of labels and functions, prompts show key names,
and the voice bus and beards are not built.
