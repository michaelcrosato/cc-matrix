# Source research

Illustrated original manual: https://odyssey2.info/library/manuals/us_kcskrazychase/K.C.%27s%20Krazy%20Chase%20%28USA%29.pdf

Viewed illustrated pages 4/5 (zero-based renders retained). The text establishes four-way wrapping, tree eating, persistent head danger, edible segments, pale frightened Drats, spinning catches, 1/3/10/20 scoring, faster later rounds, five mazes, editor and score/name entry. Tail-tip-only eligibility is historically unknown.

Gameplay: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=whykVbhZD8c (The Video Game Museum, 2021). Downloaded 20–40 seconds and inspected a five-frame contact sheet at four-second intervals, plus a frame at excerpt second 2. Observed open lanes, a visible segmented yellow train making right-angle routes, a blue keeper, sparse green tree shapes and pale frightened helpers. This establishes visual changes between sampled frames; no subjective listening is claimed. Source sound heard: unknown.

Packaging URL supplied by PLAN.md could not be fetched through the research tool. Original Radio-Electronics June 1983 PDF URL returned 403. Those observations are not claimed verified.

The game's tail-only target, bait, simultaneous co-op, multiple crawlers, finite courses and modern workshop are intentional new decisions. All in-game art/music/voices are original procedural code; no footage or manual artwork is used as game assets.
