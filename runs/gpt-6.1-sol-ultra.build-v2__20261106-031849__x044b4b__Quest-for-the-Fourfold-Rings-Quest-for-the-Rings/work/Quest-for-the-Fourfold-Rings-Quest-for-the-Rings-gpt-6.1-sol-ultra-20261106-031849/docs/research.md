# Research foundation

Research performed 2026-11-06. The historical source, engine handoff and online protocol are separate sources of evidence. Engine and Hub revision notes are in `engine.md` and `network.md`.

## Historical source

Read the complete [original manual transcript](https://odyssey2.info/library/manuals/us_questfortherings/quest-for-the-rings.txt). Inspected the [scanned manual](https://odyssey2.info/library/library_view.php?i=53), specifically its gold dragon-medallion cover, illustrated hero/monster pages, and the Dungeon, Inferno, Shifting Halls and Crystal Cavern pages with actual gameplay insets. Downloaded reference scans remain dev-only in this directory; none are game assets.

Confirmed relationships retained in the adaptation: two simultaneous, complementary heroes cooperate over a castle-route board; a ring benefits both. Warrior kills lesser foes; Wizard disables them temporarily; Phantom traverses stone/crystal but is vulnerable to lava; Changeling invisibility redirects enemies yet contact is dangerous. Crystal walls disappear, halls shift, and Inferno walls are hazardous. Spider/flying Nightmares and immortal dragons complicate escape. The human Ringmaster can possess a hero; practice can use a computer Ringmaster. Ring capture is described with lightning and trumpets. Exact original synthesis/timbres and a continuous score are unknown.

## Visual observations

The cover has a restrained warm-gold field, dark ornamental type, and a dragon medallion. Interior fantasy illustration uses layered architectural depth, vast threatening creatures and glowing magic. Gameplay insets instead use spare, vivid silhouettes on blue floors. The Dungeon illustration shows violet masonry and arches; Inferno shows orange volcanic ridges and bright molten pools; Shifting Halls shows green impossible staircases; Crystal Caverns uses blue/cyan translucent ribs. These inform original palette and spatial grammar; the game does not trace or reproduce the scans.

## Original adaptation

Four recovered rings replace ten. Modern extraction, companion AI, contextual revive/pass, bounded Dread, telegraphed hazards, original hero gear/names, possession projections and the final four-seal dragon containment are new designs. In particular, the source does not establish this game's authored eight-room campaign, final encounter, music, writing or online systems.

## Audio evidence boundary

All shipped sound is code-created Web Audio. Original music is a quiet D-minor thread with environment variations, a warmer victory resolution, and a short brass/bell recovery fanfare. Ringmaster/hero voices are formant-filtered gibberish, with readable captions supplied by the game. The source's described trumpet celebration inspired the fanfare's role; its actual original acoustic timbre was not verified. There is no claim of recreating original sound.
