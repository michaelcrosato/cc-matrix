# Boiler Buddies research

Researched before implementation on 2026-11-05.

- [Nintendo: Arcade Archives Mario Bros.](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Games/Nintendo-Switch-download-software/Arcade-Archives-Mario-Bros-1276808.html) establishes arcade platformer, 1983, pipe enemies, bump from below, simultaneous cooperative/competitive play.
- [HAMSTER, the arcade reissue publisher](https://www.arcadearchives.com/en/title/aca-060/) supplies original-screen references: black negative space, staggered single-screen brick decks, colorful pests, corner pipes, central POW, minimal HUD.
- [Atari's contemporary Mario Bros. manual, archived](https://www.atariage.com/2600/manuals_old/mario_bros.html): two-part bump/kick attack; shell 1 hit; crab 2 hits; hopper vulnerable when grounded; finite POW; coin interlude; ice and fireballs. Port details differ from arcade, so this game's six shifts are original arrangements.
- [Arcade history](https://www.arcade-history.com/?id=1564&n=mario-bros.-model-tma1-up&page=detail) used for cross-checking arcade enemies, wraparound arena, final-pest acceleration and bonus pacing.
- [Three.js official documentation](https://threejs.org/docs/) and installed 0.180.0 source verified WebGPURenderer, MeshToonNodeMaterial, AnimationMixer and keyframe tracks.
- [AI Hub Session v1](https://github.com/michaelcrosato/ai-hub/blob/main/lib/hub/README.md): exact 1.4.0 session client, authority, countdown, results and lifecycle. See hub-research.md.

Original arcade audio is sparse, with short start/clear jingles and prominent springy jumps, footstep ticks, impact and coin tones. The new game uses original synthesized swing music and squeaky formant voices rather than copies of those recordings or melodies. Direct listening in this environment: unknown.

The title was selected with Node crypto.randomInt from 50 silently brainstormed candidates. See selection.json for the selected index.
