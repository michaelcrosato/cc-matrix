The source combines a quadrant scanner with third-person combat, surface cadet rescues, fuel-limited hyperwarps, friendly repair docks, wormholes and ion-key corridors. Enemies include agile Cobra ships, heavier Mechnoids, Raiders, blockaders and flagships. Federation worlds can fall into a Red Zone with reversed controls. Its multicoloured ships, layered planetary ground, ringed planets and electronic effects shape this tribute. Exact original synthesizer patch values and musical composition are unknown; this game uses original coded music, effects and gibberish speech.

The demo condenses that loop into six authored sectors with distinct objectives. The final Sun Eater, cooperative wingmate and rechargeable nova pulse are original additions. Dialogue uses short, energetic space-adventure instructions inspired by the manual's tone. All meshes and music are new procedural work.
