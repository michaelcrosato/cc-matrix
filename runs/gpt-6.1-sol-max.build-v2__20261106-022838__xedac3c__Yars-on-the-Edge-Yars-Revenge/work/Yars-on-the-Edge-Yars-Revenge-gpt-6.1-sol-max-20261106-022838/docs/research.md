# Research ledger — 6 November 2026

## Verified relationships

- [Atari’s original-game page](https://atari.com/pages/yarsrevenge) and [the reproduced 1982 instructions](https://atariage.com/2600/manuals_old/yars_revenge.html) establish the arch and shifting rectangular shields; cells can be shot or eaten. A remote cannon aligns with the scout and can kill its operator. Vertical travel wraps. The neutral strip suppresses firing and protects from the guided missile, but not the fortress’s transformed Swirl.
- The instructions distinguish ordinary summoning by eating/touching the core from Ultimate Yars’ TRONS/left-edge rules, alternating multiplayer, and the special reflecting variation. Shooting the flying Swirl gives an extra scout. Losing all scouts ends the run; subsequent fortresses become more dangerous.
- [Howard Scott Warshaw’s interview](https://www.digitpress.com/library/interviews/interview_howard_scott_warshaw.html) establishes the intended electrical horror hum and overwhelming detonation. [Atari’s retrospective](https://atari.com/blogs/newsroom/the-addictive-and-unique-gameplay-of-yars-revenge) describes the fly embodiment, warning colors, hostile broadcast and victory explosion.
- [The pack-in comic](https://www.atarimania.com/documents/atari_comic_yars_revenge.pdf) was downloaded and its printed pages 1 and 6 inspected after rendering the scanned PDF locally. Its earnest insect civilization, cosmic-cell fortress and refuge made from a lost world inform the tone. The refuge’s weapons restriction and Swirl danger are explicit in the panels. No comic text or imagery is embedded in the game.
- [A 1982-version longplay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ngr4Fat7KZw) was located; video retrieval failed. Movement, normal/airborne cannon kills, failure and progression are verified from the original instructions and illustrations, not claimed as observed footage. The Ultimate Yars longplay was excluded as baseline evidence.

## Deliberate inventions

Nacre and Vesper are the two surviving scouts. The Bellkeeper intelligence occupies Morrow Arch, the Mirror Choir and the Last Listener. Their home, Ilex, survives as static. Five bites visibly charge a gun; simultaneous co-op, separate aiming, safe renewable core-refill docks, three authored sectors, Assist, active chains, reflective diamond cells, and the airborne-only final strike are this adaptation’s rules. Music, formant gibberish, rigs, palettes, writing, and all animations are original procedural work.
