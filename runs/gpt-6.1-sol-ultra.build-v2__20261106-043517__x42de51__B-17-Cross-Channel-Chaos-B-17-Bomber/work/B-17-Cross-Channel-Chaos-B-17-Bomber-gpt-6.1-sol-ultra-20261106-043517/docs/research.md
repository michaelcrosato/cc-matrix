# Source research — B-17 Bomber (1982)

Researched 2026-11-06. This is an original adaptation, with no affiliation to Mattel, Atari or Blue Sky Rangers. Original screenshots were inspected at the linked primary-source archives; no original pixels are included in the game or its handoff assets.

## Primary instructions

[Preserved cartridge instructions](https://history.blueskyrangers.com/instructions/b17bomber.html)

The source is a solo Intellivision/Intellivoice game. Its loop is target preview, fuel/payload preparation, takeoff, navigation, directional defense, bombing, and return to England to repair/refuel/rearm. Map destinations can be revised during flight. Targets include industry, airfields, batteries and warships; greater distance raises value. Fighters attack along four clock bearings (12/3/6/9); each has a separate mount with finite burst ammunition. Enemy damage can disable a mount until recovery. Flak may remove visible sight crosshairs while leaving bombing usable. Altering speed, bank or altitude reduces flak risk. Aircraft must retain enough fuel and airspeed to return; crossing the English coast invokes assisted landing. Missions repeat until a crash.

There are six skill levels: practice begins with 200 bombs; levels 2–6 begin with 10/8/6/4/2. Outside practice, payload can be changed within 1–17 bombs, trading fuel for ordnance. Score rewards targets, fighters and completed missions, and penalizes bomber damage and bombing England.

**Design distinction:** source emergency weight reduction jettisons fuel. PLAN's held payload-jettison action and engine extinguisher are deliberate new systems, not claimed historical mechanics.

## Developer record

[Blue Sky Rangers developer history](https://history.blueskyrangers.com/mattelelectronics/games/b17bomber.html)

The catalog description explicitly frames pilot, gunner, navigator and bombardier as changing player responsibilities. Functional radio chatter is a defining part of the tension. Credits identify Bill Fisher for sound, Phil Proctor as pilot, and Phil Austin as bombardier. The history describes a pronounced relaxed drawl in one character, not a voice style reproduced here. It also records a cut rotating turret and several rushed bugs, including incorrect rear-view flak and retained westward heading on a second sortie. The new game should use real four-direction views and explicitly reset orientation on replay.

## Visual evidence

[Preserved map screenshot](https://www.intvfunhouse.com/mattel/games/b17b-01.png) · [Preserved bombing screenshot](https://history.blueskyrangers.com/mattelelectronics/games/images/b17bomber/B17Screen.gif) · [Screenshot collection/context](https://www.intvfunhouse.com/games/b17b.php)

Viewed both images directly. The operational map uses solid blue water and green land, small contrasting symbolic targets and a cyan selection cursor. The bombing view presents a stark crosshair over block-color terrain and an industrial target with an unmistakable impact flash. These support high contrast, clear target silhouettes and concise aiming aids. New custom geometry, palette, portraits and effects are original; source pixels are not pasted into the game.

## Sound and limitations

The source's exact audio timbres, music and recordings were **not heard or audited** in this environment. Audio performance quality is unknown. Historical sound/voice credits and the functional role of warnings are confirmed by written sources; the new score, engine/gun/flak sounds and coded gibberish voices are original procedural work. A browser's synthesized samples and levels can be checked automatically, but no listener or physical hardware is available.

## Interpretation for this build

Preserve responsibility for one vulnerable aircraft, resource tradeoffs, offscreen clock calls, four defense stations, steady-run bombing and meaningful recovery. Authored route beats, optional targets, three difficulty profiles, damage emergencies, co-op, split cameras, touch assists and a 10–12 minute operation are adaptation choices. No source campaign-level structure was confirmed, so do not describe the demo operation as a recreation of an original level.
