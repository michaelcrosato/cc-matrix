## Transform consistency audit

Simulation and presentation share east/south/z-up units; `BEARINGS` provides the four actual world headings. Gun cameras attach at different physical positions and use the same yaw/pitch aim convention as shots. The view additionally transforms attachment position through bomber pitch/bank and rolls its camera. Ballistic prediction and bombs use identical fixed-step integration; full-sim impact checks establish the authoritative sight solution. The visual sights must avoid implying aim can teleport the bomb origin. Fine drift correction requires flight/route adjustment and timing the shared prediction.
