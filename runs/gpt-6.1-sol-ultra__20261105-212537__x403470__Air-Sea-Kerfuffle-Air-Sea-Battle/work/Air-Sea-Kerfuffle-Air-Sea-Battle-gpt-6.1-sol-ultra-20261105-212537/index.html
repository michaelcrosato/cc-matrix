FIELD MANUAL · READ, THEN FIRE

Man the cannon.

01

Lead your targets.

Aim ahead of aircraft. Your shells need time to arrive. Blimps block shots and score nothing.

02

Mind the waterline.

Switch to guided torpedoes for ships. Aim left or right. Mines block them — or start a chain reaction.

03

Burst. Then breathe.

Long bursts overheat the barrel. Shoot falling bombs before they reach your island. Green patrol boats repair the coast. Quick kills build a score chain.

04

Make a big fuss.

Hits charge your broadside. Unleash it to hit the fleet, clear bombs and repair 5% of the coast. Sink the flagship before 136 seconds.
