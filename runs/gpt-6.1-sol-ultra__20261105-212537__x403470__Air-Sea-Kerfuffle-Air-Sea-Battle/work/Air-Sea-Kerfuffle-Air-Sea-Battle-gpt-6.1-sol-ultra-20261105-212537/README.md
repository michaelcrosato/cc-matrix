# Air-Sea Kerfuffle

An original cel-shaded coastal arcade shooter inspired by Atari’s **Air-Sea Battle (1977)**. A full operation lasts up to 136 seconds: aircraft, convoy interception, combined arms, and a flagship showdown. Aim ahead, manage barrel heat, use guided torpedoes against ships, and charge a coast-saving broadside.

Open the long-named `.html` in the parent folder directly. Three.js r180, Rapier 0.19.3 and fonts load from CDNs, so the first load needs internet. The WebGPU renderer automatically supports WebGL2 as a fallback. All artwork, animations and audio are generated by code. Music starts at 10%.

Mouse aims; click or Space fires; A/D or arrows rotate; Q swaps weapons; E unleashes a charged broadside; Escape pauses. Standard controllers use left stick to rotate or right stick to aim, R2 to fire, L1 to swap, Triangle to broadside, and Options to pause. PlayStation/Xbox prompts are selectable. Two local gunners can use two controllers, or player two uses J/L, K, U and I. Touch drags aim and auto-fire, a second finger activates broadside, and weapons adapt to the wave.

Local co-op shares coast integrity and tracks each gunner’s score. Online co-op supports AI Hub Session Protocol v1 and independent six-character rooms with PeerJS. Public signalling availability and restrictive NAT behaviour outside the tested network are unknown.

The pause menu contains developer tools: live balance sliders, invincibility, autopilot, wave advance, flagship spawn, repair/charge, and reset. Title/menu inactivity starts a looping AI demonstration after 10 seconds. Any input returns to the title.

## Development

`node build.mjs` assembles the source modules and exact Hub client into `index.html` and the single release file in the parent folder.

`python3 test-all.py` runs the build/package checks, complete game tests, official AI Hub harness scenarios, real two-page room tests, and offline audio rendering. It owns a temporary loopback server on an OS-selected port and closes it on completion. It prints only failures and one count. The Playwright interpreter can be overridden with `PLAYWRIGHT_PYTHON`.

Research and protocol details are in `source-research.md` and `hub-research.md`. Release feedback and catalogue information are in the parent folder’s `exit-interview.json` and the in-game About screen.
