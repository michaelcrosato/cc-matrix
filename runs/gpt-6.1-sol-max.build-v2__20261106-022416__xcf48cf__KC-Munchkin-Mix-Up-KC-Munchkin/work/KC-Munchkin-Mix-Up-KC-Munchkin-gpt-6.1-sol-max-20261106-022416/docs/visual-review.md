# Visual review

Inspected rendered images rather than using screenshots as gameplay proof. Legal play and protocol evidence are separate tests.

- TV at 1920 × 1080: the complete board fits inside its cabinet surround; the native focus ring, six title actions, footer and score/life/remaining-food hierarchy are legible. Hero rings and numbers remain visible. The title footer was shortened into the safe area; an inactive toast is explicitly hidden.
- Creature distinction: cool round antenna heroes, small warm edible faces, starred power snacks, narrow Needle, pointed-eared Peek and broad Chonk read differently. Co-op uses different head details as well as colour and numbered rings.
- Phone portrait: the whole board fits the width with no joystick graphic. Menu stays clear of the stage label. Contextual chirp lives below the board and shows cooldown. Touch editor selection has a compact palette and real tools.
- Vanishing stage: boundary and pen stay visible while inner wall bodies fade. Faint landmarks and the nearby collision echo preserve orientation. The chirp restores the actual walls; it does not reveal a different collision map.
- Gate motion strip: the wind-up cue precedes the door change, the opening moves a quarter turn, and the rotor settles. The one active opening always matches the graph; no simultaneous opposite doors are drawn.
- Results, credits and workshop use the native TV Shell and its focus/scroll behaviour. Feedback is paginated rather than squeezed into one panel.
